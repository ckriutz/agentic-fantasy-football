# Bootstrap Memory Example

Use this as a structural example for a compact, durable bootstrap document. Adapt it to the agent's actual strategy and evidence. Do not copy the example's player details, and do not store a full roster, live scores, current injuries, or transaction history here.

```markdown
# Example Team Game Plan

## Identity
- **Team Name**: Example Team
- **Agent ID**: player-01

## League Settings
- Full PPR, 10 teams, 16-player rosters.
- Starting lineup: QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, TE, FLEX, K, and DEF.

## Core Strategy
### Core Philosophy
Build a reliable weekly starting core, preserve depth at volatile positions, and make transactions only when they improve an identified need.

### Draft Strategy
Favor reliable volume early, then add upside and injury-insurance depth without abandoning positional balance.

## In-Season Operating Plan
Use league tools to confirm phase, roster, players, and matchup facts. Review waivers for material upgrades, preserve locked starters, and update lineups only after confirming current roster information.

## Current Strategic Priorities
- Maintain enough RB depth for the next two bye weeks.
- Resolve a starter with a confirmed long-term absence.

## Validated Learnings
- **2026-09-22, high confidence:** Keep a viable backup at thin positions after two weeks with no usable replacement. Reassess after the bye-week cluster passes.

## Known Failure Modes
- Do not use stale bootstrap roster notes instead of `GetMyRoster`.
- Do not replace a pending waiver claim without current roster and availability evidence.

## Recent Decision Log
- **2026-09-22, Week 3:** Retained the current roster after no available player materially improved the confirmed need.
- **2026-09-15, Week 2:** Moved an unlocked bench player into a fillable starter slot after confirming the lineup and lock state.

## Memory Maintenance Rules
- Keep current priorities to 10 items or fewer.
- Keep the decision log to the 10 most recent material decisions.
- Promote a learning only after repeated evidence or a clear process failure, and include a reassessment condition.
- Use league tools, not this file, for current roster, score, injury, availability, and transaction facts.
```
